**CloudTusker Billing** is a free, open-source **WHMCS alternative for web hosting companies**. It provides billing, invoicing, client management, service provisioning, domain management, payment processing, automation, and hosting infrastructure integrations in one modular platform.

Built on the **FOSSBilling** foundation, CloudTusker Billing is being developed and extended specifically for modern hosting providers that want an open-source alternative to commercial hosting billing and automation platforms.

## Features

- **Billing & Invoicing**: Automated invoices, recurring billing, payment tracking, reminders, and transaction management.
- **Client Management**: Manage customers, contacts, services, invoices, transactions, and account information from one central platform.
- **Hosting Service Management**: Create and manage hosting products, plans, upgrades, renewals, suspensions, and cancellations.
- **Automated Provisioning**: Automate hosting service provisioning, suspension, unsuspension, and termination.
- **Domain Management**: Manage domain registrations, renewals, transfers, pricing, and domain-related services.
- **Payment Gateways**: Support multiple payment providers through a modular payment gateway architecture.
- **Product & Pricing Management**: Create hosting plans, domain products, add-ons, configurable options, and recurring pricing.
- **Hosting Control Panels**: Designed for integration with popular hosting control panels such as cPanel and DirectAdmin.
- **Automation**: Automate recurring hosting operations, billing events, provisioning, renewals, suspensions, and service lifecycle management.
- **Client Portal**: Provide customers with access to their services, invoices, domains, payments, and account information.
- **Admin Portal**: Centralised management of customers, products, services, billing, payments, and hosting infrastructure.
- **Multi-Currency**: Support multiple currencies for international hosting businesses.
- **Multi-Language**: Designed to support hosting businesses serving customers across different regions.
- **Modular Architecture**: Extend the platform with modules, payment gateways, provisioning integrations, services, and custom functionality.
- **API & Integrations**: Build integrations with hosting infrastructure, payment systems, domain registrars, automation platforms, and external applications.
- **Security**: Built with authentication, access control, permissions, and security-conscious architecture in mind.

## Why CloudTusker Billing?

CloudTusker Billing is built for hosting providers that want greater control over their billing and automation infrastructure.

It provides an open-source alternative to **WHMCS** while maintaining the flexibility required by hosting businesses operating shared hosting, reseller hosting, WordPress hosting, VPS, cloud, dedicated, application, and other hosting services.

CloudTusker Billing can be self-hosted, customised, extended, and integrated with your existing hosting infrastructure.
